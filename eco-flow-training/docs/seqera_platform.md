> 🎯 **Who is this for?** Anyone who'd like to follow their Nextflow runs in a web browser, on a laptop, in Codespaces or on an HPC, instead of watching a terminal. You'll need a free Seqera account (Step 1).

### What you'll learn

- What Seqera Platform is, and what it does (and doesn't) do for you
- How to create an access token and keep it safe
- How to send a run to Seqera Platform and explore it in the browser
- How to do the same from your HPC
- An overview of launching pipelines from the Platform itself
